About WildIndy

WildIndy isn't a tourism website. It's an adventure app for a state most people drive across without stopping.

Press the dice button, and it hands you one destination — a waterfall, a covered bridge, a haunted library, a 4,000-pound ball of paint. No itinerary, no sponsored listings, no fifteen tabs of "Top 47 Things To Do." Just one place, right now.

What's here

A hand-picked, growing list of Indiana spots across a dozen categories: waterfalls, hiking, small towns, food, scenic drives, covered bridges, museums, ghost stories, roadside attractions, nature, history, and hidden gems. Every one has a location, a driving time, a difficulty, and a map link.

How it's built

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step, no tracking. The whole thing runs off a couple of JSON files, so it loads fast and stays easy to maintain. Your bucket list and trivia score live in your browser's local storage, not on a server somewhere.

What's next

More adventures, better photos, AI-assisted trip planning, and maybe a passport you can actually stamp. For now: go press the button.
